**What it does**
AI agents that conduct two-way conversations (email, SMS, chat, messaging apps) to research/qualify leads from events, ads, and inbound inquiries, send personalized follow-ups, and book meetings; also runs customer-service and renewal/upsell conversations.

**AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it**
Two-way conversational qualification (not just one-shot outreach) is the more genuinely agentic piece, and the "resolve issues by taking action" claim (triggering workflows, updating systems) is verifiable via the platform's stack-integration positioning - though independent verification of conversation quality was not possible from public marketing pages alone.

**RevOps role**
One of the longest-established players in this category (predates the current "AI agent" branding wave by roughly a decade as a chatbot/virtual-assistant vendor); now repositioned with heavy "AI Agent" language.
