What it does
An AI sales agent that researches prospects via "Live AI search," writes and sends personalized email/LinkedIn outreach, qualifies replies, and books a meeting only once a prospect is judged ready.
AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it
Plain-English targeting instead of static list filters, and reply qualification before human handoff are the more genuinely agentic pieces; "thinks before it acts" / "refuses to spam" positioning is vendor copy not independently verified.
RevOps role
Outbound sequencing + qualification layer for teams without a human SDR at all - priced and marketed as a full SDR replacement, not a tool add-on.
