**What it does**
AI sales roleplay and "revenue activation" platform that turns an ICP description into an interactive AI buyer in minutes, covering cold calls, discovery, demos, and post-sale conversations, with call scoring against custom scorecards.

**AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it**
Genuinely AI: AI buyers built from 2M+ hours of real B2B sales conversation data hold dynamic, unscripted conversations; automatic scoring of both practice and (via Gong/Salesloft/Chorus integration) real calls against a custom methodology scorecard. Gamification (leaderboards, certification) is plain automation layered on top of the AI core.

**RevOps role**
Practice and live-call scoring layer that integrates into an existing conversation-intelligence stack (Gong, Salesloft, Chorus) rather than replacing it.
