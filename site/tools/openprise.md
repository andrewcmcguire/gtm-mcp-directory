**What it does**
A no-code RevOps data-orchestration platform automating GTM data workflows - list loading, cleansing, deduplication, enrichment, scoring/segmentation, and lead routing across the marketing/sales stack.

**AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it**
Markets "AI orchestration" for turning "AI pilots into secure, scalable, production-ready workflows" with context management, hallucination validation, and ROI tracking, but discloses no technical specifics on what is actually LLM-driven versus rule-based. Given the vagueness, treat these AI claims skeptically - the demonstrable, well-documented capability is deterministic no-code data orchestration (matching, dedup, enrichment via "App Factory"/"API Factory").

**RevOps role**
Backend data-quality and orchestration layer for RevOps - the plumbing that cleans, dedupes, enriches, and routes GTM data between CRM/MAP/other systems, rather than a front-end sales tool.
