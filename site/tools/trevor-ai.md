**What it does**
AI planning app that merges task management and calendar scheduling - connects task lists (Todoist, Google Tasks, Microsoft To-Do) and calendars (Google, Outlook, Microsoft 365) into one auto-scheduled daily plan.

**AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it**
Genuinely AI-flavored feature set - "AI-powered scheduling that predicts task duration and suggests optimal time slots," a "Plan My Day" auto-scheduler, a "personal AI model" (Pro tier), and an "Ask Trevor" conversational interface. Vendor claims a completion-rate lift ("85% vs 40%") that is unverified/self-reported and should be treated as marketing, not fact.

**RevOps role**
Personal calendar/task-optimization layer for an individual rep, same niche as Motion/Reclaim but priced for solo use.
