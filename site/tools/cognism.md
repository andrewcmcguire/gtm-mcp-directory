**What it does**
A B2B contact and company database and sales intelligence platform, positioned around GDPR/CCPA-compliant data for EMEA-focused selling, with a browser extension for LinkedIn/website prospecting and human-verified phone numbers ("Diamond Data").

**AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it**
An "AI Search" feature speeds up query-building and account research inside the product. The platform's actual headline differentiator (Diamond Data) is explicitly manual - human researchers call and verify each phone number - so the accuracy claim rests on human verification, not AI, despite AI branding elsewhere in the product.

**RevOps role**
Contact/company data provider, typically used alongside outbound tooling for EMEA/GDPR-sensitive prospecting; often positioned as a compliance-forward alternative to ZoomInfo
