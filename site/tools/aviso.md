AI revenue operations platform combining pipeline forecasting, conversation/deal intelligence, and agentic workflow automation for sales, RevOps, and customer success teams.

**AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it**
Vendor claims "advanced forecasting models" using a "time-series database" and deal win-probability scoring derived from call/email signals - plausible ML claims but no disclosed methodology or benchmark, and includes an unverified marketing claim of "nearly 100% accurate" forecasting. **RevOps role**
