**What it does**
In-app digital adoption and sales-enablement platform that surfaces contextual "Speks" (bite-sized content, process guidance, messaging) directly inside the tools reps already use (CRM, email, etc.).

**AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it**
Spekit AI / "AI Sidekick" recommends relevant content based on rep role, territory, deal, and past engagement, and answers rep questions in-line - a recommendation/retrieval layer over Spekit's own content library, not a conversational roleplay or buyer-simulation product. No dynamic AI-persona coaching found.

**RevOps role**
In-workflow enablement/adoption layer that pushes content and process guidance into the CRM and other daily tools, rather than a standalone LMS.
