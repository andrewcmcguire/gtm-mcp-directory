**What it does**
AI sales-forecasting and pipeline-intelligence platform built for HubSpot and Salesforce users, applying machine learning, time-series models, and weighted-pipeline methods to predict a forecast range (best case/commit/worst case) and support what-if scenario modeling.

**AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it**
Vendor claims "AI sales forecasting based on machine learning" targeting "up to 90-95% forecasting accuracy" and an "AI forecast corridor" predicting ranges from historical deal patterns - vendor-stated accuracy figures, not independently verified.

**RevOps role**
Standalone forecasting/pipeline-intelligence layer sitting on top of HubSpot or Salesforce, positioned as an accuracy upgrade over native CRM forecasting rather than a full RevOps platform.
