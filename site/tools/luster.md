**What it does**
"Predictive Enablement" platform combining AI cold-call/objection-handling roleplay drills with live-call signal analysis to flag rep skill gaps before they show up in real deals.

**AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it**
Genuinely AI: AI-generated coaching feedback delivered after every practice session and real call, described as personalized and consistent with the org's sales methodology; roleplay scenarios simulate real buyer interactions without a manager present. The "predictive" skill-gap forecasting layer that connects roleplay performance to live-call signals is a vendor differentiator claim that could not be independently verified.

**RevOps role**
Practice-plus-prediction layer meant to sit between roleplay drills and live-call intelligence, flagging which reps need targeted coaching before a real deal call.
