Revenue enablement platform (Bigtincan acquired Brainshark in 2021 and folded it in as "Bigtincan Readiness") spanning content management, on-demand training/coaching, and AI-driven practice.

**AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it**
Vendor names a full AI suite - RolePlayAI (dynamic practice), CoachingAI, AuthoringAI, Genie Assistant, MeetingsAI, and SearchAI - plus AI text-to-image, text-to-speech, and text generation for content creation. **RevOps role**
Combined content-management-plus-practice enablement suite; Brainshark's legacy strength (video-based pitch practice/coaching) is now one module inside the broader Bigtincan platform.
