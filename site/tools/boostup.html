What it does
AI revenue-intelligence and pipeline-forecasting platform that ingests CRM, email, call, and calendar data to produce forecasts and deal-risk scores, plus (post-rebrand) automated GTM workflow agents.
AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it
Vendor claims a proprietary "Machine Forecast" model built on a "Revenue Graph" with specific accuracy figures (e.g. 92.4% current-quarter accuracy vs. 84.1% prior) - vendor-published, no independent methodology disclosed. The newer "AI Revenue Fleet" agents (Pipeline Builder, Sales Process Agent) are largely LLM-driven workflow automation (finding contacts, drafting outreach), not predictive ML.
RevOps role
Forecasting/pipeline-inspection layer sitting on top of Salesforce/HubSpot, increasingly repositioning (as Terret) as a broader GTM agent-automation layer rather than a pure forecasting point tool.
