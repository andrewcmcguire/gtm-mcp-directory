**What it does**
Tracks job changes of a company's past customers and champions as they move to new roles/companies, and flags closed-lost opportunities for re-engagement, integrated directly into Salesforce.

**AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it**
"Insider AI" is marketed as synthesizing call transcripts, emails, and CRM data to recommend who/when/how to re-engage - plausible LLM synthesis, but no methodology is disclosed publicly, so treat as an unverified vendor claim. The core job-change detection itself is plain matching, not ML.

**RevOps role**
Champion-tracking / warm-outbound trigger tool, a near-direct competitor to UserGems with the same product shape.
