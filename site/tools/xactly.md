**What it does**
Revenue platform combining incentive-compensation management (Xactly Incent) with pipeline/revenue forecasting (Xactly Forecast), using compensation-plan and rep-behavior data as a forecasting input.

**AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it**
"Xactly Intelligence" claims bias/sandbagging detection using 20+ years of rep-behavior data, compensation-dissatisfaction signals as an early attrition-risk indicator, and deal-health scoring incorporating email sentiment and meeting frequency - more specific than most vendor "AI" claims in this file because it's tied to Xactly's genuinely unusual comp-plus-forecasting data combination, though the methodology itself isn't published. Superlative claims ("99.6% forecasting accuracy," "$7B in monthly commissions processed") are vendor-stated, not independently verified.

**RevOps role**
Incentive-compensation-plus-forecasting platform, differentiated from pure CRM-based forecasting tools by tying comp-plan behavior directly to forecast risk.
