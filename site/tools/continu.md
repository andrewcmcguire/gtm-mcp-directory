**What it does**
AI-powered enterprise learning platform for delivering, tracking, and measuring training content to employees, customers, and partners.

**AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it**
"Eddy" AI learning agent answers training questions conversationally inside Slack/Teams/SMS without switching platforms; AI-assisted content authoring (live previews, smart segmentation) and AI-powered analytics tying learning activity to business outcomes. Retrieval/generation over the org's own content library - not conversational buyer-roleplay simulation.

**RevOps role**
General-purpose corporate LMS that can serve as the enablement/onboarding backbone; less sales-specific than Mindtickle/WorkRamp.
