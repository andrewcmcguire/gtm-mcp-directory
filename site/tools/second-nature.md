**What it does**
AI roleplay platform for enterprise sales teams that generates AI buyer personas from a text description or uploaded content and runs 1:1, group, or chat-based practice conversations, then scores and gives feedback.

**AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it**
Genuinely AI: AI builds full roleplay scenarios (personas + evaluation rubric) from freeform text prompts, drives dynamic voice conversations with simulated buyer personalities across 30+ languages, and auto-scores/coaches after each session. This is the core product, not a bolt-on.

**RevOps role**
Practice/certification layer for reps, typically sitting alongside a call-intelligence tool (Gong/Chorus) rather than replacing it - practice environment vs. live-call analysis.
