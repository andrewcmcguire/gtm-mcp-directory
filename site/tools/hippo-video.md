**What it does**
Video engagement platform for sales teams to record, automate, and personalize video outreach at scale, merging prospect variables into template videos and chaining them into "Video Flows" inside sequences.

**AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it**
Vendor states an AI Script Generator, an AI Avatar Video Generator, an AI Editor that assembles finished video from a script, a "Humanize AI" layer, and automated name/image/company personalization. None independently verified beyond vendor pricing/product pages.

**RevOps role**
Video-messaging layer plugged into CRM/sequence tools (Outreach, Salesloft, HubSpot, Salesforce); also spans customer-support/CX video beyond pure prospecting.
