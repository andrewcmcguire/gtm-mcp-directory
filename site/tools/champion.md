**What it does**
Identifies and scores which existing customers are most engaged or most at risk, builds rich profiles of their expertise/preferences, and matches them to sales/CS deal contexts for reference requests, case studies, and expansion motions.

**AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it**
Markets "Revenue AI" for contact scoring (engaged vs. at-risk) and deal-context matching/ranking of champions - vendor-described on the homepage, no methodology disclosed, not independently verified.

**RevOps role**
Champion/advocate-identification and revenue-attribution layer spanning CRM, product-usage, support, and community-engagement signals - the customer-marketing sibling to job-change trackers like UserGems/Champify, but scoring champions inside existing accounts rather than tracking them to new employers.

DISAMBIGUATION FLAG - do not confuse with Champify (champify.io), a different company covered in 05-signals-intent-abm.md that tracks job changes of past customers/champions moving to NEW companies. Champion (championhq.com) instead scores and activates champions WITHIN existing customer accounts for references, case studies, and expansion - a distinct product shape despite the near-identical name and adjacent positioning. Several plausible domains for a "Champion" tool were checked and ruled out during this research: champion.io (redirects to an unrelated personal site), trychampion.com (parked/domain-marketplace listing), and champion.app (parked for sale on GoDaddy) - championhq.com is the verified, live, correct domain.
