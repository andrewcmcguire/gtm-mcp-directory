What it does
Video sales-messaging platform with a built-in lightweight CRM - record/send personalized prospecting and follow-up videos via email, SMS, LinkedIn, iMessage, or QR code, with trackable video landing pages.
AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it
Vendor markets several named AI agents: "Dubb Sales Agent" (lead gen, CRM updates, Slack inbox triage), "Ava" (AI avatar video generation from a script, multi-voice/language), "Caira" (video-performance scoring: clarity, empathy, persuasiveness), and "Otto" (in-app guidance). Consistent across the vendor features page, G2, and a third-party consulting review, but not independently verified beyond those sources.
