**What it does**
Salesforce-native AI-guided-selling platform whose forecasting product reads live Salesforce opportunity data to produce automatic rep/manager/VP forecast roll-ups without CSV exports or manual sync.

**AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it**
Conversation intelligence (auto-transcribes/scores Salesforce-dialer calls, surfaces deal risk in real time); deal-health scoring that flags forecast risk and sits "as a peer voice" alongside reps/managers rather than replacing their number with a single AI figure; sentiment analysis mapping rep sentiment trends to pipeline-stage movement. Genuinely call-derived signals, not just CRM-field math.

**RevOps role**
Salesforce-embedded forecasting-plus-conversation-intelligence layer, positioned as an always-current forecast (no separate report to maintain) rather than a standalone forecasting platform.
