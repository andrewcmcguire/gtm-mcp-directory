**What it does**
Identifies which companies visit a website via IP-to-company matching, tracks on-site and off-site behavioral/intent signals, and enriches identified companies with firmographic data and optional verified contact email/phone credits.

**AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it**
Vendor markets "AI Powered Segmentation" and "AI Powered Leads Insights" described as automatically grouping visitors by behavior/engagement/intent - no model details disclosed; reads as rules-based behavioral scoring/classification, not confirmed deep learning or LLM work.

**RevOps role**
Visitor ID + intent-based segmentation + lightweight contact enrichment feeding CRM or outbound tooling.
