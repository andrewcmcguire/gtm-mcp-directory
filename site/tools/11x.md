**What it does**
An autonomous outbound worker ("Alice") that researches leads against an ICP, writes personalized copy, and runs multi-channel sequences (email, phone, social, SMS) through to meeting booking.

**AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it**
Genuinely multi-step: signal-based prospect selection, per-prospect personalized message generation, and channel sequencing are AI-driven, not static templates. "Always learning" / 24-7 autonomy claims are vendor copy only - no independent verification found.

**RevOps role**
Outbound execution layer that sits on top of a CRM + data sources; replaces (or augments) a human SDR's prospecting-to-booking motion.
