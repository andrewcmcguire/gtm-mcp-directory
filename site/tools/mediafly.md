**What it does**
Combined revenue-enablement and revenue-intelligence platform; the InsightSquared product (acquired January 2022) is now folded into "Mediafly Intelligence360," providing deal inspection, pipeline forecasting, and conversation intelligence alongside Mediafly's original content-enablement product.

**AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it**
Vendor claims predictive insights, dynamic content personalization, and deal-analysis agents that recommend content/next-best-actions, plus ML-based content tagging. Notably, Mediafly's own AI page states MCP support is roadmap-only, verbatim: "We're also building MCP-based multi-agent orchestration as the next layer... on our roadmap" - a direct vendor admission it isn't shipped. Other "AI" features (natural-language search, FAQ assistants) read as LLM-wrapper workflow features rather than predictive ML.

**RevOps role**
Combined content-enablement plus deal/forecast-intelligence stack, spanning both what reps show buyers and how leadership inspects the pipeline.
