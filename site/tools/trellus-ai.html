What it does
A hybrid AI calling platform: a parallel dialer embedded in Salesloft/Outreach/HubSpot with real-time AI coaching for live reps, plus separate fully-autonomous voice agents for inbound call handling, appointment setting, and FAQ response.
AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it
Important and explicit distinction confirmed on the vendor's own site: outbound cold-calling is NOT autonomous - Trellus augments a human dialer with live coaching and parallel-line dialing; only inbound call handling uses fully autonomous voice agents. This is one of the more honest vendor self-descriptions found in this pass.
RevOps role
Calling/dialing augmentation layer that sits inside an existing sales-engagement platform rather than replacing one.
