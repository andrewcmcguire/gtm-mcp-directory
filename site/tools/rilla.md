**What it does**
AI speech-analytics platform for field/in-person sales (home services, insurance) that records and analyzes face-to-face conversations via a mobile app rather than video-call bots.

**AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it**
Script-compliance tracking, "virtual ridealongs," automated coaching feedback, and custom moment-flagging derived from in-person conversation audio.

**RevOps role**
Field-sales conversation-capture layer for industries without video calls; CRM sync exists (HubSpot, Salesforce, Dynamics 365, Lasso) via native integration, not a documented public API.
