**What it does**
Identified and scored anonymous B2B website visitors by matching visitor IPs/first-party signals against 30+ data sources, then triggered configurable AI-agent workflows (enrichment, research, alerting) on high-intent accounts.

**AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it**
"Agent Swarm" ran configurable AI agents doing waterfall enrichment/research on leads with feedback loops - closer to genuine LLM-agent orchestration than most competitors in this category. "Account Fit & Intent Scoring" was more conventional rules/scoring on aggregated signal data, not disclosed as deep ML.

**RevOps role**
Was a website visitor-ID + intent scoring + AI-agent-driven lead research/enrichment layer feeding sales outreach.
