**What it does**
Real-time AI video-conferencing platform for sales calls that coaches reps live against playbooks (MEDDIC, Challenger, BANT, Sandler) during the meeting.

**AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it**
Live coaching cue cards, real-time buyer-engagement and attention-drop alerts, auto-generated CRM updates, video highlight reels, pre-written follow-up emails.

**RevOps role**
Live-meeting coaching plus CRM-auto-update layer, positioned as a real-time alternative to post-call analysis tools like Gong.
