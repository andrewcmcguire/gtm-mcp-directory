**What it does**
Sales enablement and readiness platform for onboarding, content, and skill certification, with a dedicated AI Sales Role Play module for live conversation practice.

**AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it**
Genuinely AI: AI Sales Role Play uses generative AI models to run dynamic (not scripted) buyer-persona conversations that push back and ask follow-up questions, built from natural-language prompts and uploaded objections, with AI grading/feedback. The broader content-management, certification, and course-authoring core of the platform is traditional LMS/CMS functionality with AI-assisted search and recommendations layered on top - not itself conversational AI.

**RevOps role**
Enablement/onboarding hub with a genuine AI roleplay module; typically the training layer feeding readiness/certification data into a CRM.
