**What it does**
Enterprise sales-performance-management (SPM) platform covering incentive compensation, quota setting, territory design, pipeline management, and revenue forecasting in one suite.

**AI features, separated from automation with an AI label on it**
"Varicent LIFT" (Revenue Intelligence) markets pipeline analytics and coaching insights - at-risk-deal identification, rep-performance patterns, forecast risk - plus a "Revenue Intelligence Workbench" for predictive forecasts and opportunity scoring; vendor-described, no independent model/methodology detail found.

**RevOps role**
Comp-plus-forecasting suite for large, complex organizations with dedicated sales-ops teams - closer to Xactly's category than to lighter CRM-native forecasting add-ons.
